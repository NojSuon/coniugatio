What is Mati Mati?

Mati Mati is a community and web platform to teach, preserve and share Indigenous and minority languages. Today we work with languages of Mexico, and we want to add more and more regions and communities.

Learning a language means stepping into a different way of seeing the world, and doing it as a community.
